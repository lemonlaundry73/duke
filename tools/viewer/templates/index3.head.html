Try it

Lightsaber swings three times: two burns, then the ending you pick (or a random one). Shoot him picks a blaster death (sag to the knees and topple) or a headshot (limp, falls forward or back); every death hands over to a physics ragdoll. Shoot his foot makes him limp. Thermal detonator lands near him and picks one of four blasts, some taking an arm or a leg. Roll & fire, Ladder and Mantle show the movement set. Tiptoe sneak walks on the balls of his feet; the gesture row plays the head look, neck roll, counting to five, the index-finger come here and the tiptoe peek. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. Turn sound on first.
